# Hablemos

A personal Spanish trainer for moving to Mexico. The goal is to be conversational in 6 months and fluent in 12.

**Live app:** https://claude.ai/artifact/C1B1frUVgKhjZvwdHAVczD

## What's inside

- **Lección:** one guided 90-minute lesson a day in six timed steps. It saves as you go, so you can stop and continue later the same day.
- **Tarjetas:** spaced-repetition flashcards of Mexican Spanish phrases, verbs, connector words and expressions, with audio.
- **Leer:** readings at your level written by Claude, with tap-to-translate words and comprehension questions.
- **Conversar:** an AI tutor ("Lupita") who acts out real situations in Mexico and corrects your Spanish.
- **Ruta:** a 12-month roadmap in five phases, with goals to tick off.

## How it runs

`index.html` is the source of the app published on claude.ai. Progress, the AI tutor, AI-written readings, voice and the mic come from claude.ai, so use the live link above. Opened anywhere else, the page still runs the flashcards, the three included readings and the plan, but progress is saved only in that browser.

## Updating

Ask Claude to change the app, giving it the live link. Then copy the new version into `index.html` and commit it here.
